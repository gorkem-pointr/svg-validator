SVG Validator & Alignment

A browser-based toolkit for working with Vusion / Pointr floor-plan SVGs. Validate the structure of an SVG against the geolocation API, or align its anchor points onto a real-world map.

Validate

Check the SVG's internal structure and its consistency with the geolocation API.

Align

Drop the SVG onto an OpenStreetMap tile, drag the anchors, save the corrected lat/lon back into the file.

Get started:
  1. Pick an SVG in the sidebar.
  2. Click Validate or Align.

Validation

Run structural checks and (optionally) cross-check against the geolocation API.
